Dwarkadhish Temple Complete Travel Guide

Welcome to the complete travel guide for Dwarkadhish Temple, one of the most famous temples of Lord Krishna in India.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Dwarka, Gujarat
  • Deity: Lord Krishna
  • Temple Type: Char Dham Pilgrimage Site
  • Best Time To Visit: October to March
  • Darshan Fee: Free

Temple Timings

Morning: 6:30 AM to 1:00 PM
Evening: 5:00 PM to 9:30 PM

Temple View

Dwarkadhish Temple is a sacred Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Krishna, located on the western coast of Gujarat.

History of Dwarkadhish Temple

Dwarka is believed to be the ancient kingdom of Lord Krishna. The temple has great religious and historical importance.

Best Time To Visit

  • October to March (best weather)
  • Janmashtami festival (very crowded but special)
  • Early morning darshan for peaceful experience

How To Reach

  • Nearest Railway Station: Dwarka (2–3 km)
  • Nearest Airport: Jamnagar (130 km)
  • Well connected by buses from Ahmedabad, Rajkot

Travel Tips

  • Carry light clothes and water
  • Avoid peak festival crowd if you want peace
  • Footwear not allowed inside temple area

Nearby Attractions

  • Bet Dwarka
  • Rukmini Devi Temple
  • Gomti Ghat
  • Dwarka Beach
  • Sudama Setu

Tips For Visitors

  • Visit early morning for a peaceful darshan.
  • Keep footwear at designated counters.
  • Carry drinking water during summer.
  • Plan extra time during festivals and weekends.

How To Reach Dwarkadhish Temple

  • Nearest Railway Station: Dwarka Railway Station (2–3 km from temple)
  • Nearest Airport: Jamnagar Airport (approx 130 km)
  • Bus: Regular GSRTC buses available from Ahmedabad, Rajkot and Jamnagar
  • Local transport: Auto-rickshaws easily available from station and bus stand

Hotels & Stay Options

  • Budget dharamshalas available near temple area
  • Mid-range hotels available in Dwarka city
  • Advance booking recommended during weekends and festivals
  • Some hotels offer sea-view rooms near Dwarka beach

Food Guide

  • Pure vegetarian food is available everywhere
  • Gujarati thali is the most popular meal option
  • Small street food stalls available near temple area
  • Simple satvik food is served in many dharamshalas

Crowd Guide

  • Best time for peaceful darshan: Early morning (6:30–8:00 AM)
  • Heavy crowd days: Weekends and festivals
  • Very heavy crowd: Janmashtami festival
  • Summer afternoons are less crowded but hot

Travel Tips

  • Carry light and comfortable clothes
  • Keep extra time during festival days
  • Mobile phones may have restrictions inside temple
  • Keep water bottle during summer visits

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is entry free? Yes.
  • Is parking available? Yes.
  • Best time to visit? October to March.
  • Can senior citizens visit easily? Yes.
