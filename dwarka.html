Dwarkadhish Temple Guide

Complete Travel Information for Visitors

Overview

Dwarkadhish Temple is a sacred Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Krishna, located in Dwarka, Gujarat. It is one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Dwarka, Gujarat
  • Deity: Lord Krishna
  • Temple Type: Char Dham Pilgrimage Site
  • Best Time: October to March
  • Entry: Free

Temple Timings

  • Morning: 6:30 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Evening: 5:00 PM – 9:30 PM

Temple View

Dwarkadhish Temple is one of the most sacred temples of Lord Krishna in India.

How To Reach

  • Nearest Railway Station: Dwarka
  • Nearest Airport: Jamnagar (130 km)
  • Well connected by buses from Gujarat cities

Hotels & Stay

  • Budget dharamshalas available
  • Mid-range hotels in city area
  • Sea view hotels near beach

Food Guide

  • Pure vegetarian food available
  • Gujarati thali is popular
  • Satvik food in dharamshalas

Crowd Guide

  • Best time: Early morning
  • Heavy crowd: Festivals & weekends
  • Janmashtami: Extremely crowded

Travel Tips

  • Carry water in summer
  • Avoid peak rush hours
  • Respect temple rules

Nearby Attractions

  • Bet Dwarka
  • Rukmini Temple
  • Gomti Ghat
  • Dwarka Beach
