Somnath Temple Complete Travel Guide

Welcome to the complete travel guide for Somnath Temple, one of the most sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Prabhas Patan, Gujarat
  • Deity: Lord Shiva
  • Temple Type: Jyotirlinga
  • Best Time To Visit: October to March
  • Darshan Fee: Free

Temple Timings

Opening Time: 6:00 AM
Closing Time: 9:30 PM

Aarti Timings

  • Morning Aarti: 7:00 AM
  • Noon Aarti: 12:00 PM
  • Evening Aarti: 7:00 PM

Nearby Attractions

  • Triveni Sangam
  • Bhalka Tirth
  • Geeta Mandir
  • Somnath Beach

Tips For Visitors

  • Visit early morning for peaceful darshan.
  • Use lockers for restricted items.
  • Wear comfortable clothes.
  • Carry water during summer.

Parking Information

  • Parking facilities are available near the temple complex.
  • Two-wheeler parking available.
  • Car parking available.
  • Festival days may have heavy parking demand.
  • Arrive early during weekends and festivals.

Mobile & Camera Rules

  • Mobile phones are generally not allowed inside the main temple area.
  • Cameras are not permitted.
  • Power banks are restricted.
  • Locker facilities are available.
  • Footwear counters are available.

One-Day Suggested Itinerary

  • 8:00 AM - Breakfast
  • 9:00 AM - Triveni Sangam
  • 11:00 AM - Geeta Mandir
  • 1:00 PM - Lunch
  • 2:00 PM - Bhalka Tirth
  • 5:00 PM - Evening Temple Visit
  • 8:00 PM - Light & Sound Show

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is entry free? Yes.
  • Is parking available? Yes.
  • Are mobile phones allowed? Restrictions apply.
  • Best time for darshan? Early morning.
  • Can senior citizens visit easily? Yes.

Hotels & Dharamshala

  • Budget hotels are available near the temple area.
  • Various dharamshalas serve pilgrims.
  • Mid-range hotels are available in Somnath and Veraval.
  • Book in advance during Mahashivratri and major festivals.

Food Guide

  • Vegetarian restaurants are widely available.
  • Gujarati Thali is a popular choice.
  • Tea, snacks and South Indian food are easily available.
  • Drinking water facilities are available in many areas.

Crowd Guide

  • Lowest crowd: Early morning.
  • Moderate crowd: Weekdays.
  • Heavy crowd: Weekends.
  • Very heavy crowd: Shravan month and Mahashivratri.
  • For peaceful darshan, arrive before 6:30 AM.

History of Somnath Temple

Somnath Temple is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. It is traditionally regarded as the first Jyotirlinga and has been rebuilt multiple times throughout history. The present temple stands proudly on the Arabian Sea coast and is one of India's most important pilgrimage destinations.

Emergency & Safety Information

  • Carry a valid ID proof.
  • Follow temple security instructions.
  • Keep children close during crowded periods.
  • Use official parking and locker facilities.
  • Save emergency contact numbers before travel.
