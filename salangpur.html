Kashtabhanjan Hanuman Temple Complete Travel Information

Overview

Salangpur Hanuman Temple (Kashtabhanjan Hanuman Ji) is one of the most powerful Hanuman temples in India, located in Gujarat. It is famous for removing negative energies, fear and obstacles from devotees' lives.

Quick Facts

  • Temple Name: Kashtabhanjan Hanuman Ji Temple
  • Location: Salangpur, Botad District, Gujarat
  • Deity: Lord Hanuman
  • Temple Type: Hindu Temple
  • Famous For: Removal of negative energies & protection
  • Best Time To Visit: October to March
  • Entry: Free

History of Salangpur Temple

The temple is dedicated to Kashtabhanjan Hanuman, a powerful form of Lord Hanuman. It is believed that Lord Hanuman removes fear, evil spirits, and obstacles from devotees' lives.

How To Reach

  • Nearest Airport: Ahmedabad Airport (approx 100–120 km)
  • Well connected by road from Ahmedabad, Bhavnagar, Rajkot
