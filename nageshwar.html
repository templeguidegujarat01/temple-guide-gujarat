Nageshwar Jyotirlinga Temple Guide

Complete Travel Information for Visitors

Overview

Nageshwar Jyotirlinga is one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, located near Dwarka in Gujarat. It is a highly powerful and spiritual Shiva temple and is visited by thousands of devotees every year.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Near Dwarka, Gujarat
  • Deity: Lord Shiva
  • Temple Type: Jyotirlinga
  • Best Time To Visit: October to March
  • Entry: Free

History of Nageshwar Jyotirlinga

According to Hindu mythology, Nageshwar Jyotirlinga represents protection against negativity and evil forces. It is believed that Lord Shiva protects devotees from all dangers at this sacred place.

How To Reach

  • Nearest Railway Station: Dwarka (approximately 15 km)
  • Nearest Airport: Jamnagar (approx 130 km)
  • Well connected by road from Dwarka and Gujarat cities

  • Pure vegetarian food available everywhere

Crowd Guide

  • Best time: Early morning
  • Heavy crowd: Weekends & festivals
  • Very crowded during Shivratri

Travel Tips

  • Carry water in summer
  • Visit early morning for peace
  • Respect temple rules
  • Avoid rush hours on weekends
